# S1 — Scope (15 min)

Goal: one role, one boundary. Create nothing else.

## Questions

1. What's the one role you want this to run? If you name several, I'll push
   back — pick the one with a weekly rhythm.
2. What does a good week in that role look like?
3. What does a bad week look like?
4. What do you re-explain to an AI every single time you open a new chat?
5. What information do you keep losing?
6. Is this role one YOU own, or one your employer partly defines?
   (Corporate: steer toward the former. "My weekly reporting and stakeholder
   prep" survives a reorg; "the Q3 migration" does not.)
7. What must never go in these files?
   Corporate framing: "What are you not authorised to point an agent at?
   Client files, HR material, anything under NDA." Record as off-limits.
8. Anything personal you want kept out of anything syncable?

Symlink CLAUDE.md → AGENTS.md so the folder works in both ecosystems.

## GATE
One role named. Off-limits list recorded. Do not proceed on three roles —
say: "Three roles is three systems. Which one has a weekly rhythm? We build
that one, and you'll know how to build the others."
