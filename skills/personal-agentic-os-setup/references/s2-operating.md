# S2 — Operating interview (~40 Q, 40 min)

How they actually work. Not how they write — that's S3.

## Categories

CADENCE (1-6)
What does your week look like, hour by hour, on a normal week? What's the
first thing you look at on Monday? What recurs weekly? Monthly? What always
slips? When in the week do you do your best thinking?

RECURRING DECISIONS (7-13)
What decisions do you make over and over? Walk me through the last one.
What information do you want in front of you when you make it? What have you
decided before and then forgotten you decided? What do you second-guess?
What do you decide too fast? What do you avoid deciding?

PEOPLE (14-21)
Who do you deal with repeatedly? For each of the top five: what do they
care about, what do they not want to hear, how do you talk to them, what
should an assistant remember about them? Who do you dread? Who unblocks you?

ARTIFACTS (22-28)
What do you produce over and over? Paste the last one. What's the structure
you always use? What takes longest? Where does the output go? Who reads it?
What makes a good one versus a passable one?

FAILURE MODES (29-34)
What breaks in a bad week? What do you drop first under pressure? What do
you do badly and know it? Where do you procrastinate? What do you redo
because you lost the earlier version?

DELEGATION BOUNDARY (35-40)
What could an assistant do without asking you? What needs approval? What
should it never touch? What would you be embarrassed to have it get wrong?
If it could prepare one thing for you every morning, what?
