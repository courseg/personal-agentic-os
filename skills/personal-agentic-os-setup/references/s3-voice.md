# S3 — Voice interview

## Scale first
"Does this role produce writing other people read?"
  Centrally (writer, marketer, academic, exec comms) → full count
  Incidentally (emails, updates) → 40% of count
  Barely (logistics, health, ops) → 20 Q, then gate and move on

Count is set by tier in preflight: frontier 100 / mid 40 / local 25.

## Categories, in order

1. ORIGIN — how you learned to write, what you read, who you imitated,
   what you've deliberately unlearned
2. MECHANICS — sentence length, paragraph shape, punctuation habits, how
   you open, how you close, what you always cut on the second pass
3. VOCABULARY — words you overuse, words you'd never write, jargon you
   accept vs. jargon that makes you cringe
4. STRUCTURE — how you build an argument, how you handle disagreement,
   how you deliver bad news
5. REFUSALS — the cringe list. Push hardest here. 15 minimum.
   "What makes you close the tab? What would you never publish? What does
   everyone in your field do that you refuse to?"
6. JUDGEMENT — three things you believe that most of your field doesn't
7. REGISTER — how you shift between audiences, what never shifts
8. SAMPLES — two or three real pieces you're proud of, one you aren't.
   Paste them, don't describe them.

Every captured item gets HARD RULE / STRONG TENDENCY / LIGHT PREFERENCE.

## Write TWO files

  context/interviews/voice-full.md
    Verbatim Q&A, unsummarised. Archive. Read only when recalibrating.

  context/voice.md
    Under 600 words. Hard rules first, refusal list second, tendencies
    third, three sample sentences last. This is what AGENTS.md points to.

In the user's working language, both of them.

## GATE
15+ refusals. If they only gave preferences, do not pass:
"You've told me what you like. I still don't know what you'd refuse to
publish — that's the part that makes this steerable. Give me ten things
that make you cringe."
